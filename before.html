Getting into Indonesia

Neither a New Zealand nor an Australian passport gets you in without a visa. Sort these before you fly and immigration takes minutes — leave them and you're in the slow queue.

Getting a phone working

Use an eSIM, not a local SIM card. Indonesia makes foreign phones register their IMEI before a physical SIM will work, which can take days and sometimes just fails. Travel eSIMs skip that entirely.

Will your phone take one?

Dial *#06# on the keypad. If an EID number appears under the IMEI, you're fine. No EID means no eSIM.

Rough rule: iPhone XS or XR (2018) and newer, Samsung Galaxy S20 and newer, Google Pixel 3 and newer. The phone also has to be carrier-unlocked — worth ringing your provider if you're not sure.
